How it works

From delivery to stock removal in three steps.

Register and receive in Central

Each product gets a code (001, 002…) and a quantity per box. Enter boxes, batch and expiry date; Central records everything in units.

Transfer for daily use

Move only what is needed to Operational. Both sides are recorded.

Remove stock on your phone

Your team records the removal and the system chooses the batch that expires first.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install an app?

No. Argos opens in your phone’s browser and can be added to the home screen, where it works like an app.

What if someone records the wrong quantity?

Movements cannot be edited or deleted. Corrections use a positive or negative adjustment with a required reason, keeping the history transparent.

Can stock go negative?

No. The system blocks removals above the available balance, even when several people record movements at once.

Is my data separated from other companies?

Yes. Each company’s data is isolated, and access is checked directly in the database.

Will Argos alert me when something needs attention?

Yes. You can set alerts for products nearing expiry or running low, and Argos notifies you when the condition occurs.
